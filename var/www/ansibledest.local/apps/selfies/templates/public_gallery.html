About This Portal

The Griffin Creek Trestle Selfie Portal is part of an experimental community WiFi project. Visitors to our location can connect to a local network that serves this interactive photo-sharing experience, fostering connection and creativity in our neighborhood.

This is a live, read-only view into that local community space. Photos are shared voluntarily by visitors and automatically curated for public viewing. The network creates an offline-first community experience that brings neighbors together through technology.
